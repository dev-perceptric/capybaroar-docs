Everything AI in Capybaroar draws from one credit balance: the agent, writing help, pictures, videos and voiceovers.

<LinkButton href="https://app.capybaroar.com/settings?tab=billing" variant="secondary" icon="external">Open in Capybaroar</LinkButton>

## Credits on each plan

| Plan | AI credits a month |
| - | - |
| Free | None |
| Creator | 1,000 |
| Pro | 2,500 |
| Agency | 8,000 |
| Pro trial (14 days) | 200 |

Plan credits refill on your billing date each month, on yearly plans too. Unused ones don't carry over.

## What things cost

| Action | Credits |
| - | - |
| Rewrite, fit to a network, suggested reply or caption | 4 |
| An AI thread, X poll, LinkedIn carousel or TikTok slideshow | 10 |
| Build a link in bio page with AI | 40 |
| An agent message | What it really used, at least 2. Long jobs, like a week of posts with pictures, use more. |
| Pictures, videos and sound | Priced by the model and length. A short video costs the most. |

1,000 credits make about 125 images, or about 6 weeks of posts planned by the agent. If something fails, you get its credits back. No single action costs more than 1,000 credits.

## Buying more

On a paid plan you can buy packs: 1,000 credits for $12, 5,000 for $50 or 20,000 for $160. Bought credits last 12 months and are used after your plan credits. You can also turn on automatic top-up, which buys a pack when your balance gets low.

<Steps>

1. **Open Billing.** Click **Settings**, then **Billing**.

1. **Buy a pack.** Under **Buy credits**, pick a pack and pay.

1. **Turn on top-up if you like.** Switch on **Automatic top-up** and pick the pack. It buys one when your balance drops under 200 credits.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Only owners and admins can buy credits or change the plan.
</Aside>

## See where credits go

**Credit history** in **Settings**, **Billing** lists every AI action with its credits, the person and the model. Press **Download CSV** to keep a copy.

## Limits per client

On Agency you can cap how many credits each client brand uses a month, so one client can't use up the whole pool. Set it in the **Credit limit** column under **Settings**, **Brands**. Leave it empty for no limit.
