Capybaroar has an MCP server, so the AI you already use can plan, write and schedule posts in your account. It is on every plan, Free too.

<LinkButton href="https://app.capybaroar.com/settings?tab=api" variant="secondary" icon="external">Open in Capybaroar</LinkButton>

## Claude: sign in

Claude connects with your Capybaroar sign-in, so there is no key to copy.

<Steps>

1. In Claude, open **Settings**, then **Connectors**.

1. Press **Add custom connector**, name it Capybaroar and paste `https://app.capybaroar.com/api/mcp`

1. Press **Connect**, sign in to Capybaroar, pick the workspace and press **Allow**.

1. Start a new chat.

</Steps>

In Claude Code, run this and sign in when it asks:

```sh
claude mcp add --transport http capybaroar https://app.capybaroar.com/api/mcp
```

Apps you connect this way are listed in **Settings**, **API and MCP**, under **Connected apps**. Press **Remove** to cut one off.

## Other apps: copy your MCP link

<Steps>

1. In Capybaroar, open **Settings**, then **API and MCP**.

1. Press **New key** and copy the **MCP link**. It looks like `https://app.capybaroar.com/api/mcp/cbr_...`

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
Keep the link private. Anyone with it can post as you. You only see it once, so make a new key if you lose it.
</Aside>

## ChatGPT

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings**, then **Apps and Connectors**.

1. Under **Advanced**, turn on **Developer mode**. This needs a paid ChatGPT plan.

1. Press **Create**, name it Capybaroar and paste your link.

1. In a new chat, open **+** and pick Capybaroar.

</Steps>

## Cursor

Open **Cursor Settings**, then **MCP**, press **Add new MCP server** and paste this with your link:

```json
{
  "mcpServers": {
    "capybaroar": { "url": "https://app.capybaroar.com/api/mcp/cbr_your_key" }
  }
}
```

## Try it

- "Use Capybaroar. Look at my channels and recent posts, then write a week of posts about [topic]. Save them as drafts at my next queue times."
- "Use Capybaroar. List everything scheduled for this week."
- "Use Capybaroar. Show my results for the last 30 days and tell me which posts did best."

## What your AI can do

List channels, brands and channel groups; create, update, list and delete posts; find the next queue time; upload and list media; make pictures and videos (with an MCP link); read analytics and credits; and manage webhooks. The [REST API](/developers/rest-api/) page lists every action in detail.

| Tool | What it does |
| - | - |
| `list_brands` | The brands (clients) in the workspace |
| `list_channels` | Connected channels with their character limit and the settings each network takes |
| `list_channel_groups` | Your channel groups |
| `list_campaigns` | Your campaigns |
| `list_posts` | Posts in a time range, up to 92 days |
| `get_post` | One post, with its status and link on each channel |
| `create_posts` | Up to 25 posts at once, as drafts unless asked to schedule |
| `update_post` | Change a post that hasn't gone out |
| `delete_post` | Delete a post from Capybaroar, or on the networks too with `everywhere: true` |
| `list_media` | Files in the media library, to attach by id |
| `upload_media` | Add a picture or video from a public link |
| `generate_media`, `get_generation` | Make an infographic, animation, picture, video or audio, and check on it |
| `get_credits` | Your AI credits left and when plan credits refill |
| `next_queue_slot` | The next free time in the posting queue |
| `get_analytics` | Reach, engagement, best times to post and top posts |
| `list_webhooks`, `create_webhook`, `set_webhook`, `test_webhook`, `delete_webhook` | Manage webhooks (owners and admins) |

<Aside type="note">
Everything your AI does goes through the same checks as the app: your role, your brands, your plan's limits and approvals.
</Aside>
