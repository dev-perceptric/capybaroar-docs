This page explains the words you see in Capybaroar, grouped by where they show up in the app.

## Channels and brands

| Term | What it means |
| - | - |
| Channel | One social account Capybaroar can post to, like your Instagram account or your LinkedIn Page. |
| Network | The service a channel is on, like Instagram or Bluesky. Capybaroar posts to 14 networks. |
| Brand | One client, product or company in your workspace. Each brand has its own channels, calendar, Inbox, results and link in bio page. |
| Workspace | Everything your team shares: brands, channels, posts, members and the plan. You can belong to more than one. |
| Channel group | A saved set of channels you post to together, picked from the **Group** menu in the editor. |
| Reconnect | A channel's network signed Capybaroar out. Click **Reconnect** and sign in again. Scheduled posts stay in place. |
| Active | A channel that is connected and ready to post. |
| Brand kit | A brand's voice, words to avoid and hashtags, which AI follows when it writes for that brand. |

## Posts and the calendar

| Term | What it means |
| - | - |
| Post | One piece of content, sent to one or more channels. Each channel can have its own text and settings. |
| Draft | A saved post with no time to go out. Drafts don't count toward the Free plan's 30 posts a month. |
| Scheduled | A post set to go out at its time. |
| In review | A post waiting for approval before it can be scheduled. |
| Partly published | A post that went out on some channels and was turned down on others. |
| Failed | A post that didn't go out on any channel. Open it to see why. |
| Queue | The posting times you set in **Settings**, **Queue**. **Add to queue** puts a post in the next free one. |
| Best times | The days and hours your posts get the most engagement, once 10 posts have results. |
| Campaign | A name that ties posts together, like a launch, so you can filter the calendar by it. |
| First comment | A comment Capybaroar posts right after the post, often used for links or hashtags. |
| Thread | Several posts that go out as replies to each other, on X, Threads, Bluesky and Mastodon. Up to 25 posts. |
| Evergreen | A published post that goes out again as a queued copy every few weeks. |
| Bulk | Making many posts at once from a CSV file or a stack of pictures and videos. |
| Feed | A blog's RSS or Atom feed that turns new articles into drafts or queued posts. |
| Delete everywhere | Taking a post down on each network it went out on, from Capybaroar. |
| Short link | A link Capybaroar shortens so it can count clicks. |

## Teams and approvals

| Term | What it means |
| - | - |
| Owner | The person who made the workspace. They can do everything, including deleting it. |
| Admin | Runs the workspace: settings, billing, members, brands and keys. |
| Editor | Writes and schedules posts for their brands. Their posts can need approval. |
| Client | Opens the calendar for their brands to approve or reject posts. Clients don't take a paid seat. |
| Seat | A place for a team member on your plan. Owners, admins and editors take one. |
| Posts need approval | A brand setting that sends editors' posts to review before they're scheduled. |
| Review link | A link a client can open, with no account, to approve or reject a brand's posts. |

## Results

| Term | What it means |
| - | - |
| Reach | How many people saw a post, as the network counts it. |
| Engagements | Likes, comments, shares, saves and other actions on a post. |
| Engagement rate | Engagements as a share of reach. |
| Report | A brand's results for a period, as a link, a PDF or an email that goes out weekly or monthly. |
| Link in bio | One page at your own address with your links, latest posts and videos, with visits and clicks counted. |
| Inbox | One list of the comments, replies and mentions from your channels. |

## AI and developers

| Term | What it means |
| - | - |
| The agent | Claude with every Capybaroar action. It plans and writes posts, makes media and reads your results, in **Agents**. |
| Capy | The name for what makes pictures, videos and sound in Capybaroar, like CapyImage and CapyVideo. "Capy picks" means it chooses the model. |
| AI credits | The one balance every AI action draws from. Plans refill it each month, and you can buy packs. |
| Automatic top-up | Buys a credit pack on its own when your balance drops under 200 credits. |
| MCP server | The way AI apps like Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor work in your Capybaroar account. |
| MCP link | A link with your key in it, for AI apps that take only a link. Keep it private. |
| API key | A key starting with `cbr_` that lets your code act as you through the REST API or MCP. |
| Webhook | A POST Capybaroar sends to your URL when an event happens, like a post going out. |
