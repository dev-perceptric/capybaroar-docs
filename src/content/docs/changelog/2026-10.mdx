## October 31: the new Capybaroar

The new Capybaroar went live on October 31, 2026, rebuilt from the ground up. It shipped with five sections in the sidebar:

- **Calendar.** Every post for every channel, shown by channel. Write a post once, schedule it, and Capybaroar sends it at its time, trying again by itself when a network has a hiccup.
- **Channels.** Connect your social accounts. Each network is written from that network's own official API.
- **Agents.** Chat with the AI agent to plan and write posts. It saves them as drafts for you to check.
- **Analytics.** See how your posts did.
- **Media.** Keep your pictures and videos in one library, ready for any post.

Sign in with email and password, or with Google where it's turned on.

### Fixed

- The agent page no longer breaks when a long chat scrolls into view.
