## Channels

| Network | What you can post | Connects with |
| - | - | - |
| [Instagram](/channels/instagram/) | Posts, Reels and Stories | Sign in with Meta |
| [Facebook](/channels/facebook/) | Posts, Reels and Stories on Pages | Sign in with Meta |
| [TikTok](/channels/tiktok/) | Videos and photo posts | Sign in with TikTok |
| [LinkedIn](/channels/linkedin/) | Posts and PDF carousels, on profiles and Pages | Sign in with LinkedIn |
| [YouTube](/channels/youtube/) | Videos | Sign in with Google |
| [X](/channels/x/) | Posts, threads and polls | Sign in with X |
| [Threads](/channels/threads/) | Posts and threads | Sign in with Meta |
| [Pinterest](/channels/pinterest/) | Pins | Sign in with Pinterest |
| [Bluesky](/channels/bluesky/) | Posts and threads | Handle and app password |
| [Mastodon](/channels/mastodon/) | Posts and threads | Your server, then sign in |
| [Discord](/channels/discord/) | Messages | A webhook link |
| [Telegram](/channels/telegram/) | Messages in a channel or group | The Capybaroar bot as an admin |
| [WordPress](/channels/wordpress/) | Blog posts | Site address and application password |

## Three ways to use Capybaroar

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="In the app">

Sign in at [app.capybaroar.com](https://app.capybaroar.com). Connect a channel, then write your first post in the calendar.

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="From your AI app">

Connect Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor to the Capybaroar MCP server and ask for posts in plain words. It is on every plan, Free too. See [Use Capybaroar from Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor](/ai/mcp/).

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="With the API">

Make posts and read results from your own code. Get a webhook when a post goes out. See the [REST API](/developers/rest-api/) and [Webhooks](/developers/webhooks/).

  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Your first ten minutes

<Steps>

1. **Create your account**

   Sign up and start 14 days of Pro for free. [Create your account](/get-started/create-account/)

1. **Connect a channel**

   Add the social account you post from. [Connect your first channel](/get-started/connect-a-channel/)

1. **Schedule a post**

   Write it once, then pick a time. [Schedule your first post](/get-started/schedule-your-first-post/)

1. **Let the agent plan your week**

   Ask for a week of drafts in one message. [Plan a week of posts](/ai/plan-a-week/)

</Steps>
